ANALYSIS 
======================================
While this new method is mathematically 94% faster, in absolute terms it only saves 6.8 milliseconds per run. 
Therefore, this optimization is only 'efficient' if this function is executed millions of times per day. 
For a single-use or low-frequency task, the improvement is negligible and not worth the complexity.
